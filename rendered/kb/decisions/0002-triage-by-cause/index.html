Replacement triage procedure

  1. Collapse repeated report IDs. Preserve the original body and time fields.
  2. Resolve the tenant from the directory. Check its actual locale and booking form.
  3. Compare causes using the channel and tenant configuration, not the subject.
  4. Same known cause: merge into that cause’s ticket. Different causes: keep separate.
  5. Unknown tenant or unplaced cause: investigate; never merge simply because subjects match.
  6. Recompute counts and re-read the evidence when the desk or tenant configuration changes. Keep this export’s scope distinct from a claim about the live queue.
